# snugdoku-site

Legal site of Snugdoku (privacy, terms, support; English and Turkish): https://beydadonmezz.github.io/snugdoku-site/

Since 2026-10-23 this is the single legal site of the merged Snugdoku app (Place, Connect, Divide). The pages are
generated by `legal/build.py` in the app repo (beydadonmezz/snugdoku); do not edit them here by hand. The old
snugpath-site pages and the beydadonmezz.github.io/quiltdoku/ pages redirect here (`legal/redirects.py`).
`404.html` and `assets/site.css` are kept from the earlier site.
