Snugdoku

A cosy logic puzzle. Place one piece in every row, every column and every coloured region. Pieces never touch, not even diagonally.

What's inside

  • Hand-made logic: every puzzle has exactly one solution and can be solved by deduction alone, no guessing.
  • A level road from 4x4 to endless 10x10, and a daily puzzle that everyone in the world solves on the same day.
  • Hints that explain the next logical step, cute pieces and colour themes, light and dark mode.
  • 11 languages. For iPhone, iPad and Android.
