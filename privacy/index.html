Privacy Policy

Snugdoku · Effective date: 17 October 2026

The short version

  • Snugdoku has no account and no sign-up. We never ask for your name, email address, phone number or location.
  • Your progress is saved on your device. We do not run our own server for your data, and we do not use our own analytics, crash reporting or tracking tools.
  • Ads are provided by Google AdMob. They are personalised only if you agree (on iPhone and iPad in Apple's tracking prompt; in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland also in Google's consent form). Otherwise you get non-personalised ads.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple or Google. Leaderboards and achievements are handled by Apple Game Center or Google Play Games.
  • The game works the same whatever you choose in any privacy prompt.

1. Who we are

Snugdoku is made by BEYDANUR DONMEZ (Beydanur Dönmez) ("we", "us"), the data controller for the app. Contact: snugdoku.sandbox@outlook.com. Google and Apple provide some services inside the app (ads, purchases, leaderboards); they handle the data they collect under their own privacy policies, linked below.

2. Data on your device

The game saves on your device: your level progress and best times, daily puzzles you solved and your streak, hints used today, your settings (theme, colour, piece, sound, vibration, language) and whether you bought Remove Ads.

So that deleting and reinstalling the game on the same device does not reset today's free hints or your streak, a small copy of that progress (hints used today, the days you solved the daily puzzle, your highest level and the Remove Ads flag) is also kept in your device's iOS Keychain (on iPhone and iPad, not synced through iCloud Keychain) or in Google Block Store (on Android). It stays on your device and in your own device backups; it is never sent to us. On Android, the game's progress is also included in Android's own backup of your phone if you use it.

3. Ads by Google AdMob

The game shows a full-screen ad after some finished levels (never during the first three tutorial levels or on the daily puzzle), and an optional rewarded ad you can watch for an extra hint. Ads are served by Google AdMob, which collects device and usage data such as your IP address, device type, operating system, app version and ad interactions, and, if you allow it, your device's advertising identifier, to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud. See how Google uses data from apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Your privacy choices

  • iPhone and iPad: the game asks for permission to track through Apple's prompt. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", ads are non-personalised. You can change it any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: Google's consent form appears before ads load. You can change your choices any time in the game: Settings → Ad privacy choices.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Google → Ads).
  • Remove Ads (an in-app purchase) turns off the full-screen ads. Rewarded ads stay available because you start them yourself.

5. In-app purchases

Remove Ads is sold through the App Store or Google Play. Apple or Google process the payment; we never see your payment details. The game only learns whether you own Remove Ads, so it can turn the ads off and restore the purchase.

6. Game Center and Google Play Games

If you are signed in to Apple Game Center (iPhone, iPad) or Google Play Games (Android), the game sends your solve time for the daily puzzle (only your first completion of the day) and your highest level to their leaderboards, and reports achievements. Your player name, avatar and these scores are visible to other players as set in your Game Center or Play Games profile. Apple or Google handle this data under their own policies; we only see the leaderboards as any player does.

7. Daily puzzle downloads

To get new daily puzzles without an app update, the game may download a puzzle file from our website on GitHub Pages. Like any web request, this exposes your IP address to GitHub, which hosts the site; the request contains no personal data and we do not log it.

8. Children

Snugdoku is a game for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. Ads are limited to content suitable for general audiences.

9. Keeping and deleting data

Deleting the game removes its saved progress from your device. The small copy in the iOS Keychain or Google Block Store stays on the device (so a reinstall keeps today's hints and your streak); resetting the device erases it. We hold no data about you that we could delete, except emails you send us, which we delete when we no longer need them to answer you.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or Turkey's Law No. 6698, KVKK), you have rights to access, correct, delete and object to the processing of your personal data, and to complain to a data protection authority. Because we do not hold data about you, most requests are about Google's or Apple's processing; contact them through the links above, or write to us and we will help.

11. This website

This website uses no cookies, no analytics and no external resources. GitHub, which hosts it, may log technical data such as IP addresses.

12. Changes to this policy

We will update this page and its effective date when the game's data handling changes.
