Privacy Policy

Snugdoku · Effective date: 19 October 2026

The short version

  • Snugdoku has no account and no sign-up. We never ask for your name, email address, phone number or location.
  • Your progress and diamonds are saved on your device and, so they come back after a reinstall or on a new device, in your own iCloud or Google backup. We never receive them. We do not run our own server for your data, and we do not use our own analytics, crash reporting or tracking tools.
  • Ads are provided through Google AdMob, together with the advertising partners named in section 3. They are personalised only if you agree (on iPhone and iPad in Apple's tracking prompt; in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland also in Google's consent form). Otherwise you get non-personalised ads.
  • Purchases (Remove Ads and diamond packs) are handled by Apple or Google. Leaderboards and achievements are handled by Apple Game Center or Google Play Games.
  • The game works the same whatever you choose in any privacy prompt.

1. Who we are

Snugdoku is made by BEYDANUR DONMEZ (Beydanur Dönmez) ("we", "us"), the data controller for the app. Contact: snugdoku.sandbox@outlook.com. Google, Apple and the advertising partners named in section 3 provide some services inside the app (ads, purchases, leaderboards); they handle the data they collect under their own privacy policies, linked below.

2. Data on your device and in your backups

The game saves on your device: your level progress and best times, daily puzzles you solved and your streak (including days kept with "Save your streak"), hints used today and in the current puzzle, your diamond balance, the chapters, pieces and colour themes you unlocked, the past daily puzzles you opened and solved, how many free-diamond ads you watched today, the store's transaction identifiers of the diamond packs you bought (so that no purchase is ever counted twice), your settings (theme, colour, piece, sound, vibration, language) and whether you bought Remove Ads.

So that deleting and reinstalling the game, or moving to a new device, does not lose your progress or reset today's limits, a small copy of that progress (the items above except your settings and best times, plus the time it was saved) is also kept:

  • iPhone and iPad: in your device's iOS Keychain (on this device only, not synced through iCloud Keychain) and in iCloud key-value storage, which Apple keeps in your own iCloud account when you are signed in to iCloud and syncs to your devices with the same Apple Account.
  • Android: in Google Block Store, once for this device and once in your Google account's Block Store backup (Google encrypts it end to end when your device has a screen lock). Android's own backup of your phone also includes the game's progress if you use it.

These copies live on your device and in your own Apple or Google account; they are never sent to us and we cannot read them. When the game finds a copy, it keeps the higher progress, the stricter daily limits and, only if the copy is newer, the higher diamond balance; a purchase is never added twice.

3. Ads

The game shows a full-screen ad after some finished levels (never during the first three tutorial levels or on the daily puzzle), and optional rewarded ads that you start yourself: one for an extra hint (once per puzzle) and one for 20 free diamonds (up to five a day; the day changes at midnight Türkiye time). A reward is given only after the ad has played to the end. Ads are requested through Google's Mobile Ads SDK (Google AdMob). Through AdMob mediation, these advertising partners can also bid to show an ad, using their own SDKs, which are part of the app:

  • Unity Ads (Unity Technologies): privacy policy
  • Liftoff Monetize (Liftoff Mobile, Inc.): privacy policy

Google and these partners collect device and usage data such as your IP address, device type, operating system, app version and ad interactions, and, if you allow it, your device's advertising identifier, to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud. They receive it directly from their SDKs; it does not pass through us. See how Google uses data from apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Your privacy choices

  • iPhone and iPad: the game asks for permission to track through Apple's prompt. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", ads are non-personalised. You can change it any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: Google's consent form appears before ads load. You can change your choices any time in the game: Settings → Ad privacy choices.
  • United States: where a state privacy law applies, Google's form lets you opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information (in the game: Settings → Ad privacy choices). The game passes that choice on to the advertising partners in section 3.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Google → Ads).
  • Remove Ads (an in-app purchase) turns off the full-screen ads. Rewarded ads stay available because you start them yourself.

5. In-app purchases

Remove Ads and three diamond packs are sold through the App Store or Google Play. Apple or Google process the payment; we never see your payment details. The game only learns which product you bought, the store's identifier of that transaction and that it is valid, so it can turn the ads off, add the diamonds to your balance exactly once and restore Remove Ads.

6. Game Center and Google Play Games

If you are signed in to Apple Game Center (iPhone, iPad) or Google Play Games (Android), the game sends your solve time for the daily puzzle (only your first completion of the day) and your highest level to their leaderboards, and reports achievements. Your player name, avatar and these scores are visible to other players as set in your Game Center or Play Games profile. Apple or Google handle this data under their own policies; we only see the leaderboards as any player does.

7. Daily puzzle downloads

To get new daily puzzles without an app update, the game may download a puzzle file from our website on GitHub Pages. Like any web request, this exposes your IP address to GitHub, which hosts the site; the request contains no personal data and we do not log it.

8. Children

Snugdoku is a game for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. Ads are limited to content suitable for general audiences.

9. Keeping and deleting data

Deleting the game removes its saved progress from your device. The small copy in the iOS Keychain or Google Block Store stays on the device (so a reinstall keeps your progress and today's limits); resetting the device erases it. The copy in iCloud key-value storage or in your Google account's Block Store backup stays in your account until you remove the game's data there (on iPhone: Settings → your name → iCloud) or delete the account's backups. We hold no data about you that we could delete, except emails you send us, which we delete when we no longer need them to answer you.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or Turkey's Law No. 6698, KVKK), you have rights to access, correct, delete and object to the processing of your personal data, and to complain to a data protection authority. Because we do not hold data about you, most requests are about Google's or Apple's processing; contact them through the links above, or write to us and we will help.

11. This website

This website uses no cookies, no analytics and no external resources. GitHub, which hosts it, may log technical data such as IP addresses.

12. Changes to this policy

We will update this page and its effective date when the game's data handling changes.
