How to play

Place one piece in every row, every column and every coloured region. Pieces may not touch, not even diagonally. Tap a cell once for an X, twice for a piece, a third time to clear it; drag across cells to mark many Xs.

Questions

How do hints work? A hint shows the next logical step and why. You get 3 free hints a day (they refresh at midnight Istanbul time, UTC+3); watch an optional ad for one more.

When does the daily puzzle change? At 00:00 Istanbul time (21:00 UTC). Everyone in the world gets the same puzzle. Only your first solve of the day goes to the leaderboard.

I bought Remove Ads on another device. Settings → Restore Purchases, signed in to the same Apple Account or Google Account.

How do I change the language? The game follows your device language. You can also pick one in Settings → Language.

The ranking is empty. Sign in to Game Center (iPhone, iPad) or Google Play Games (Android).
